About Bites

A neighbourhood spot at Arena Cinema.

Bites is a restaurant in Bahria Town Phase 4, right at Arena Cinema. It is a place to sit down after work, bring the family, or catch dinner before or after a film.

We keep the menu simple and satisfying: paratha platters, chicken tikka parathas, an all-you-can-eat option and happy-hour plates. When it gets cold, the fireplace does the rest.

Pick up the phone or send a WhatsApp message, and we will help you with your table or your order.

  • All-you-can-eat option for big appetites
  • Happy-hour food in the evenings
  • A fireplace for cosy winter nights
  • Dine-in, pickup and delivery
  • Easy to find inside Arena Cinema
